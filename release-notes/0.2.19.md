## 新功能

- **设置窗口改成左侧导航**：通用、侧边栏、舞台、所有窗口、例外、关于六页排在窗口左边，页名固定在内容上方；窗口变窄时导航只留图标。
- **每行只有标题和控件**：说明文字和各选项的含义收进标题后 ⓘ 的悬停提示，一页读下来就是两列整齐的标题和开关，需要解释时再看。
- **“所有窗口”独立成页**：概览的三种打开方式（Win+Tab、侧边栏底部的面板、屏幕角落的按钮）、面板的不透明度和模糊、强调色都集中在这一页。
- **打开“所有舞台”时，台前的组也能拆**：右键台前那张“幽灵”卡片可以拆开它；应用先都留在舞台上，下一次切换时各回各的槽位。

## 改进

- **例外应用只在设置里维护**：卡片右键菜单不再出现“打开时保留当前应用”，统一在设置 → 例外里添加程序，列表也更好看清有哪些。

## 修复

- **文件资源管理器重新有卡片和飞行动画**：之前完全不给 explorer.exe 的窗口拍静帧，它们只能显示成图标卡片、没有缩小放大的动画；现在和别的窗口一样。

## New

- **The settings window lists its panes down the left**: General, Sidebar, Stage, All Windows, Exceptions and About, with the pane's name over its content; in a narrow window the names drop and the icons stay.
- **Every row is one line**: what a setting does and what each of its choices means moved into the tip of an info mark after its title, so a pane reads as two even columns of titles and controls, with the words there when wanted.
- **All Windows has a pane of its own**: the three ways into the overview (Win+Tab, the panel at the bottom of the sidebar, the button in the screen's corner), the panel's opacity and blur, and the highlight color are all here.
- **Splitting the on-stage group with "list every stage" on**: right-click the on-stage ghost card to break it apart; the apps stay on the stage and each takes a slot of its own at the next switch.

## Improved

- **Exceptions live in Settings only**: the "keep the current app when opened" item is gone from the card's right-click menu; add programs under Settings → Exceptions, where the list is also easier to read.

## Fixed

- **File Explorer windows get their cards and flights back**: explorer.exe's windows were no longer captured, which left them as icon cards with no zoom animation; they now behave like every other window.
