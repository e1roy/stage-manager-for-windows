## 新功能

- **舞台窗口列表**：舞台上有多个窗口时，侧边栏顶部从上到下列出它们的实时小缩略图，标上序号 1/N，当前窗口高亮；每个小框的底色取自窗口自身的主色（排除黑、白、灰），同一应用的多个窗口（比如标题栏颜色不同的 VS Code 窗口）一眼就能分开。点一下移到最前，鼠标移上去可以直接关掉，右键能把它的应用收进侧边栏。可在“设置 → 侧边栏”里关闭。
- **上一个舞台的虚线框**：刚离开舞台的那张卡片会套上虚线框，从 A 切到 B 忙完之后，一眼就能找回之前在 A 上做的事。同样可以在“设置 → 侧边栏”里关闭。
- 两个开关默认都开，升级后不需要重新设置。

## New

- **A list of the stage's windows**: when the stage holds more than one window, small live thumbnails of them are listed top to bottom at the top of the strip, numbered 1/N with the active one highlighted. Each tile takes the main color its window shows (black, white and gray left out), so the windows of one app, such as VS Code windows with different title bar colors, tell apart at a glance. Click one to bring it to the front, hover to close it, or right-click to send its app to the strip. It can be turned off in Settings → Strip.
- **The stage you just left**: its card gets a dashed outline, so after a detour to another app you can see at a glance what you were in the middle of. Also turned off in Settings → Strip.
- Both are on by default; nothing to set up after the update.
