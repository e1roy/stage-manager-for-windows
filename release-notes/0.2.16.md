## 新功能

- **毛玻璃概览面板**：侧边栏底部的“所有窗口”面板改成毛玻璃，不透明度和背景模糊程度可以在设置里调。
- **面板与左下角按钮合体**：侧边栏在左侧、任务栏居中时，面板和显示器左下角的按钮合成一个紧贴屏幕左边和下边的圆角大按钮，一起显示、一起亮、一起按下。
- **在侧边栏里关掉应用**：鼠标移到卡片上，点右上角的圆形 ×；几个应用组合成的卡片会把它们的窗口全部关掉。
- **上一个舞台的虚线框跟随主题色**：更亮、间距更大，在深色背景上更容易看清（主题色关闭时仍为白色）。

## New

- **Frosted overview panel**: the "all windows" panel at the bottom of the strip is now frosted glass; its opacity and background blur can be set in Settings.
- **Panel joined to the corner button**: with the strip on the left and a centered taskbar, the panel and the button in the display's bottom-left corner become one rounded button flush with the screen's left and bottom edges, shown, lit and pressed together.
- **Close an app from the strip**: hover a card and click the round × at its top right; a card made of several apps closes all their windows.
- **The dashed outline of the stage you just left follows the accent color**: brighter and further out from the card, so it reads better on a dark background (still white when the accent is turned off).
