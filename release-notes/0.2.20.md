## 新功能

- **同一应用的窗口分开放**：在“设置 → 舞台”打开（默认关）后，同一应用的每个窗口单独一张卡片、单独一个舞台，点一下卡片只切回这一个窗口，不再先把整个应用的窗口都叫回来。应用新开的窗口单独占舞台，原来的窗口收进侧边栏；打开开关时，舞台上同一应用的其他窗口会立刻飞进侧边栏。
- **仍然合并窗口的应用**：开关下面的列表里的应用照旧把所有窗口合成一张卡片，默认是资源管理器。可以从正在运行的应用里选、浏览 exe 添加，也可以在卡片右键勾选“合并此应用的窗口”。

## New

- **Separate windows of the same app**: turn it on in Settings → Stage (off by default) and every window of an app gets a card and a stage of its own, so one click on a card brings back just that window rather than every window of its app. A new window of an app takes the stage alone and the one before it goes to the strip; turning the option on sends the other windows of the app on the stage to the strip at once.
- **Apps that keep their windows together**: the apps listed under the option still keep all their windows on one card, File Explorer by default. Add them from the apps that are running or from an exe, or check "Keep this app's windows together" on a card's right-click menu.
