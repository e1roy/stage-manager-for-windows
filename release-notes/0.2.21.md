## 新功能

- **最小化即收进侧边栏**：开启“隐藏非台前窗口”后，最小化舞台上的窗口会把该窗口所在的应用组合整体收进侧边栏，而不是只留给任务栏。
- **Win+D / Win+M 收起所有舞台**：所有受管理显示器的舞台窗口一起进侧边栏，不激活任何窗口；再按一次 Win+D 不会还原，点卡片才回来。关闭“隐藏非台前窗口”后，两个快捷键恢复系统原生行为。

## New

- **Minimize to the strip**: with inactive windows hidden, minimizing a window on the stage sends its whole stage group to the strip instead of leaving it to the taskbar.
- **Win+D / Win+M clear every stage**: managed stages all tuck into their strips without activating anything; pressing Win+D again keeps them tucked away until you pick a card. Turning off inactive-window hiding restores the native shortcuts.
