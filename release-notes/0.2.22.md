## 变化

- **最小化默认和 Windows 一致**：开启“隐藏非台前窗口”时，最小化台前窗口会进任务栏；舞台上没有别的窗口了，就换回上一个舞台。
- **新设置“最小化时收进侧边栏”**（默认关）：打开后，最小化会把整个舞台组合收进侧边栏并换回上一个舞台。点标题栏的最小化按钮或任务栏按钮时，窗口不会先缩进任务栏再飞出来。
- **舞台窗口列表**：应用图标移到小框外侧的下角，与卡片一致，缩略图不再盖住它。

## Changes

- **Minimize works as in Windows by default**: with inactive windows hidden, minimizing a window on the stage sends it to the taskbar; if no other window is left on the stage, the previous stage comes back.
- **New setting "Minimize sends the stage to the sidebar"** (off by default): when on, minimizing sends the whole stage group to the strip and brings back the previous stage. Clicking the title-bar minimize button or the taskbar button no longer makes the window shrink into the taskbar first.
- **Stage window list**: the app icon moves to the tile's outer bottom corner, as on the cards, clear of the thumbnail.
