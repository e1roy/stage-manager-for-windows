## 新功能

- **侧边栏列出所有舞台（可选）**：设置 → 侧边栏新增“侧边栏列出所有舞台，包括台前的”（默认关）。打开后，台前的应用在侧边栏里保留自己的位置，切换舞台时卡片不再互换位置、整列乱跳。
  - 台前的那张卡片画成半透明带灰度的“幽灵”，右下角带绿色对钩；不透明度和灰度都可以在设置里调。
  - 点这张幽灵卡片，它会左右轻震一下，表示当前打开的就是它。
  - 离开舞台的应用回到它原来的位置（而不是被点的那张卡片的位置），新应用追加到最下面；位置不在可视区里时侧边栏自动滚过去。
- **分组拆分按“舞台”算**：舞台上的几个应用是一组，右键拆分只对侧边栏里的组合生效；“上一个舞台”的虚线框也不会再套在台前那张卡片上。

## New

- **The strip can list every stage, the one on stage included** (optional): Settings → Strip has a new "list all stages in the strip" switch, off by default. With it on, the app on stage keeps its own slot in the strip, so the cards no longer swap places and shuffle the whole column on every switch.
  - Its card shows as a translucent, grayed ghost with a green check at the bottom right; opacity and how gray it is are in the settings.
  - Clicking that ghost shakes it a little to say that this is the one already open.
  - An app leaving the stage returns to its own slot (not the clicked card's), new apps join at the bottom, and the strip scrolls to a slot out of view.
- **Splitting works by stage**: the apps on the stage are one group, so right-click split now only applies to groups in the strip, and the dashed outline of the stage just left no longer sits on the on-stage card.
